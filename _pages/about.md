I am a second year PhD student at the University of Bristol, supervised by [Matthew Tointon](https://tointon.neocities.org/). My research mainly concerns studying groups from both a geometric and probabilistic perspective. In particular, I am currently studying how geodesics behave in groups of polynomial growth. I am also intersted in studying percolation on groups.

I am currently an organiser for the 26th Edition of [PGTC](https://sites.google.com/view/pgtc2025/) and will be an organiser for [Bristol Junior Geometry Seminar (BRIJGES)](https://sites.google.com/view/brijges/home) from Autumn 2025.
